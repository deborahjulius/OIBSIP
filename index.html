About Deborah

Hi, I'm Deborah — your French teacher and friend. I started learning French as a beginner too, so I know exactly how it feels to be confused, shy, and stuck.

That's why I created this page: to teach French in a simple way, in English and Pidgin where needed, so you actually understand and remember.
